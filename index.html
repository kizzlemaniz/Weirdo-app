07 / DRAWING VIDEO LAB

Watch ball movement

EXPERIMENTAL

Use the official drawing pages in the archive above to find past draw details, or upload a video you have permission to use. For an uploaded video, this app measures visible frame-to-frame motion while it plays on your iPhone. Open a date in the Drawing Research section to link these measurements to an official result. It cannot reliably identify every moving ball or forecast the winning numbers.
